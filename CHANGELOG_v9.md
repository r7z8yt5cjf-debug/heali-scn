# SCN Operations Manual — Version 9 (eff. August 12, 2026)

Source: §0 "Updates from Prior Versions" of the official OHIP SCN Operations Manual v9 PDF (the manual's own change log). Reproduced here for reference inside the app repo.

## SCN Infrastructure
- **Accessible training** — Added guidance on providing training in an accessible format, when needed.
- **Interpersonal Safety (IPS) training** — Added guidance on IPS training.
- **Member complaints** — Added guidance for SCNs to make their public complaint process visible on the main page and accessible on their websites.

## Care Delivery Approach
- **Asthma remediation** — Added a decision tree to help Navigators better understand how to refer members to Asthma Remediation services.
- **HRSN Transportation questions** — Added follow-up questions for HRSN transportation services to better understand members' unmet needs.
- **Household member definition and documentation** — Clarified the definition of household members and added a requirement for supporting documentation.
- **Navigators as enrollment assistors** — Added guidance on billing and training requirements for Navigators who assist members with the Medicaid re-enrollment process.
- **Community Transition Supports (CTS)** — Clarified that CTS services can be provided in increments.
- **HPNAP nutrition guidance** — Added recommendations on following national and state nutrition standards in delivery of nutrition services.
- **Nutrition vouchers** — Added guidance that nutrition vouchers must be individually assigned, non-transferable, and traceable to the eligible member.
- **Nutrition Vouchers** — Clarified that Nutrition Vouchers are not permitted to be distributed in the form of a gift card.
- **Household guidance for Medically Tailored Meals** — Added guidance on nutrition services for additional MMC-enrolled individuals in a household of a member receiving Medically Tailored Meals.
- **Age-appropriate serving sizes for Nutrition services** — Added guidance that delivery of Nutrition services should reflect differences in caloric/nutritional needs for children and adults.
- **RDN and CDN role in confirming household size** — Added guidance requiring RDNs and CDNs to confirm household members.
- **HRSN Transportation services** — Clarified that HRSN transportation services are intended to enable members to access other Enhanced HRSN Services, including but not limited to IPS supports and initial connections to employment and education services.
- **Change in member eligibility** — Added guidance for Navigators on handling changes in members' eligibility. (§5.H retitled to "Changes in Member Medicaid Eligibility, Coverage and Relocation.")
- **Proactive outreach for screening** — Added best practices on conducting outreach to members to facilitate screening.
- **Service duration for members of same household** — Added guidance to ensure members of the same household adhere to appropriate service durations.
- **Duplicative services** — Clarified guidance around determining duplicative services.

## Payments
- **Gift cards** — Clarified that gift cards cannot be used for any reason under the 1115 waiver.
- **PMPM payment reconciliation** — Clarified that OHIP is the exclusive entity that reconciles PMPM payments and payments for HRSN services rendered, and that MCOs may only recoup the OHIP-defined final reconciliation total from SCNs for the reconciliation period in scope as directed by OHIP.

## Contract Requirements
- **Provider compliance** — Added guidance on HRSN Service Provider oversight, including HRSN Service Provider Responsibility Reviews for contracted partners receiving $100,000 or more over the waiver period.
- **SCN Contract Requirements with MCOs** — Added a requirement that SCN Lead Entities contract with every MCO in New York State by November 13, 2026.
- **HRSN Service Provider Performance** — Added guidance on HRSN Service Provider compliance with performance expectations and SCN response to performance issues.

## Fee Schedule
- **Household-level Caps for Food Prescriptions and Pantry Stocking** — Added caps for household sizes 2–10 for Food Prescription and Pantry Stocking services.
- **Household guidance for Medically Tailored Meals** — Added guidance on nutrition services for additional MMC-enrolled individuals in a household of a member receiving Medically Tailored Meals.
